Peltier generator bench setup

Hot water heats the bottom of the module and ice water cools the top. The module drives a load resistor, and the Arduino reads ΔT from the two face sensors and V through the ADS1115. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom.

drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan

Stack, bottom to top

  1. ~90 °CBeaker of hot water (500 mL) with an aluminium block standing in it, top 1 cm above the rim
  2. hotAluminium plate 80×80×3 mm, hot DS18B20 taped at the corner, beside the module
  3. 3.8 mmTEC1-12706 module, 40×40 mm, thermal paste on both faces, red and black leads
  4. coldAluminium plate 60×60×3 mm, cold DS18B20 taped at the corner
  5. ~2 °CSteel cup of ice water, held lightly by the retort-stand clamp for even pressure

On the bench

  • Terminal block with the load resistor (1-100 Ω), close to the module
  • ADS1115 on the breadboard, A0 and A1 across the load
  • Arduino Uno with the 20×4 I2C LCD showing ΔT, V, I and P
  • Laptop over USB, logging CSV and plotting P vs R

Sizes are approximate. Keep the electronics well away from the hot water, and keep the leads to low-value loads short.
